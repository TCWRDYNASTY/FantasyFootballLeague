Tanking Meter

A season-long, evidence-based look at lineup decisions across all 14 teams. Every team is evaluated independently. A low score is normal; a high score signals that the commissioner may want to review the evidence.

HOW THE INDEX WORKS

Each team begins at 0.0. The index increases only when an automated check identifies a qualifying lineup decision. Scores accumulate during the season and are capped at 10.0. No team is penalized simply for having a poor record or a weak roster.
